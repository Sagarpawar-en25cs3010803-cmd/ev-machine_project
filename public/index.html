Battery Technology Breakdown

Understanding the five major battery chemistries powering the EV revolution

LFP
Lithium Iron Phosphate

Industry-leading thermal safety and cycle life. Dominant in mass-market & commercial segments. 25–30% lower cost than nickel chemistries.

⚡ Range: 350–480 km 🛡️ Safety: 4.9/5
NMC
Nickel Manganese Cobalt

High energy density ideal for performance sedans and premium SUVs. Delivers 500–600 km range but at higher cost.

⚡ Range: 500–640 km 🛡️ Safety: 4.2/5
Solid-State
Solid-State Battery

Next-generation technology offering 600+ km range and 10–18 min ultra-fast charging. Near-zero thermal runaway risk.

⚡ Range: 620–750 km 🛡️ Safety: 4.95/5
NCA
Nickel Cobalt Aluminum

Preferred by luxury and performance brands for ultra-high energy density. Tesla's original chemistry of choice for flagship vehicles.

⚡ Range: 520–650 km 🛡️ Safety: 4.0/5
Sodium-Ion
Sodium-Ion

Cobalt-free and lithium-free chemistry offering outstanding cost efficiency for budget and urban mobility segments.

⚡ Range: 280–380 km 🛡️ Safety: 4.8/5
